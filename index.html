      Import & export

      Paste a list written on your phone or computer, one product per line (you can include quantity and price), and they'll be created automatically. You can also copy your current list as text.

      Processed on your device

      Download your list as a clean PDF to print or share, or import products by reading the text of an existing PDF.

      Processed on your device

      Export your list as a clean image to share on WhatsApp, or take a photo of a handwritten list or receipt and the app will try to read the products (it may take a moment and isn't always perfectly accurate).

      Processed on your device

      A complete, exact backup of your list, in a technical format (JSON) meant for you, not for sharing. Unlike the other options, importing here replaces your entire current list.

      Send

      Generate a code on this device to send your list to another one, over the internet — no files, no account.
